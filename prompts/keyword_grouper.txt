- label: the member phrase that best names the group, copied from its members.
- keywords: the member phrases, copied exactly as listed.
- rationale: one short sentence.
- Most papers have 4 to 8 subject groups and 1 or 2 method groups; put a paper's instruments and analyses together in one method group unless they are clearly separate.
