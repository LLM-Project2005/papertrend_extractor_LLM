### ROLE ###
You map the headings of a research article, thesis or report to its standard sections.

### INPUT ###
Below is a numbered outline of the lines that look like headings, in document order, followed by the opening of the document. Headings may be English or Thai, numbered, in capitals, or chapter-based (Chapter 3, บทที่ 3).

### TASK ###
For each section, return the number of the outline line (the N in "LN") where that section starts, or 0 if the paper has no such section:
- abstract: Abstract / บทคัดย่อ
- introduction: Introduction, Background of the study, Rationale / บทนำ
- literature_review: Literature review, Theoretical framework, Related studies / ทบทวนวรรณกรรม
- methods: Methodology, Methods, Research design, Participants and procedure / วิธีดำเนินการวิจัย
- results: Results, Findings / ผลการวิจัย
- discussion: a Discussion that is separate from the results / อภิปรายผล
- conclusion: Conclusion, Implications, Recommendations / สรุปผล
- references: References, Bibliography / บรรณานุกรม
- back_matter: where acknowledgements, author notes or appendices begin after the main text

### RULES ###
1. Choose the heading that starts the section in the body, not a table-of-contents entry.
2. Choose the top-level heading of a section, not one of its sub-headings.
3. When one heading names two sections ("Results and Discussion"), give it for the first section and 0 for the second.
4. Use only line numbers that appear in the outline.
