## THE FOUR TYPES

Type 1 - Descriptive & Explanatory
Collects or analyses data to describe, compare or explain a phenomenon as it is: surveys, interviews, observation, corpus or document analysis, case studies, correlational or comparative studies. Nothing is designed or tested as the contribution.

Type 2 - Intervention & Design
Designs, builds, implements or evaluates an intervention, programme, course, system, tool or design, and asks whether it works or how it performs. The intervention or artefact is the contribution.

Type 3 - Measurement & Method
Develops, validates or critically examines an instrument, measure, scale, test, model or research method. The instrument or method is the primary deliverable or object of study.

Type 4 - Review, Theory & Critique
Reviews or synthesises literature, proposes or refines theory or a conceptual framework, analyses policy, or offers a critical or position argument without a primary empirical contribution.

---

## BOUNDARY RULE

If an instrument is used only to measure whether an intervention worked, choose Type 2. If the instrument itself is designed, validated or critiqued as the main contribution, choose Type 3.
